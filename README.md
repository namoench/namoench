# Nick Moench

**CTO & Principal Machine Learning Architect** at Quality Assured Artificial Intelligence

Building production AI systems for regulated industries. I combine hands-on ML engineering with deep biopharma domain expertise to deliver compliant, scalable AI solutions.

---

## About Me

I architect and deploy end-to-end machine learning platforms—from specifying and building multi-GPU infrastructure to developing distributed serverless applications. My background in pharmaceutical quality assurance (cGMP, FDA compliance, ISO 13485) gives me a unique perspective on implementing AI in validated, regulated environments where reliability and auditability aren't optional.

Previously, I worked in biopharma manufacturing and quality systems at Resilience, where I led cross-functional technical initiatives and developed deep expertise in regulatory compliance frameworks.

---

##  Currently Working On

- **Regulatory AI Platform** — Distributed serverless architecture for AI-powered compliance analysis across DSCSA, ISO 13485, Cell & Gene Therapy, and Aseptic Processing domains
- **Multi-GPU LLM Infrastructure** — Custom vLLM controller with automatic tensor parallelism for 70B+ parameter models across multiple GPUs
- **Document Intelligence Pipeline** — Vision-language model systems for processing regulatory documents, extracting structured data from PDFs, and automated compliance analysis
- **Vector Search Architecture** — Multi-provider vector database integrations for semantic search across regulatory documentation
