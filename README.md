## About Me

I architect and deploy end-to-end machine learning platforms—from specifying and building GPU infrastructure to developing distributed serverless applications. My background in pharmaceutical quality assurance (cGMP, FDA compliance, ISO 13485) gives me a unique perspective on implementing AI in validated, regulated environments where reliability and auditability aren't optional.

Previously, I worked in biopharma manufacturing and quality systems at Resilience, where I led cross-functional technical initiatives and developed deep expertise in regulatory compliance frameworks.
